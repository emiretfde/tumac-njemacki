Vehicle registration and car document translations

Need vehicle paperwork translated for a sale, purchase or administrative procedure in Montenegro or Germany? Sead Crnovršanin translates vehicle documents between German and Montenegrin, Bosnian or Serbian. A certified translation can be requested when required.

Request a quote ↗

Which documents can you enquire about?

Explain which documents you have and where you intend to present them. Typical enquiries include:

  • Vehicle registration certificates and associated registration papers.
  • German Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I and Teil II (Fahrzeugschein / Fahrzeugbrief).
  • Vehicle purchase agreements, invoices and supporting paperwork when a translation is necessary.

How to request a quote

  1. Tell us the document type, source and target languages, and which institution will receive the translation.
  2. Provide a readable copy only if necessary to assess the request. Both sides of registration documents may be relevant.
  3. Specify your deadline and whether the receiving authority requires certification. Pricing, timing and handover will be agreed individually.

Does every vehicle document need certification?

Not necessarily. Requirements depend on the process and the receiving institution. Ask the relevant authority whether a standard or certified translation is required, and whether originals or copies are needed. A sworn translator can certify a translation within their authorization, but cannot guarantee acceptance by a third-party authority.

Collection and delivery

Collection can be arranged in Podgorica, Bar, Bijelo Polje and Rožaje, with courier delivery available by agreement throughout Montenegro. Details are confirmed before work begins.
