05 / Frequently asked questions

Good to
know.

Quick answers to common questions before ordering a translation.

How do I request a quote? +

Contact us by email or phone, specifying the document type, target language and deadline. Avoid sending sensitive documents unnecessarily.

Can translations be certified? +

If you require a certified translation, mention this in your enquiry so the appropriate requirements can be confirmed.

What determines price and turnaround? +

Document type, length, complexity, certification requirements and deadline. You will receive a specific quote after your enquiry.

How will I receive my translation? +

Collection arrangements, including locations or courier delivery, are agreed before work begins.
